Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 October 2026

Service scope

AUD00 Backup Chromebook is a personal, single-user backup workflow based on rclone, not a public cloud-storage service.

Google Drive data

The intended OAuth permission is read-only Google Drive access. The account holder may use it to download selected documents and file metadata for backups and integrity checks. Effective permissions must be checked in the local rclone configuration before publishing the OAuth app.

Storage and retention

Backup files are stored on local or removable storage under the account holder's control. The account holder decides how long to retain them and can delete them. Authorization tokens are managed by the local rclone configuration and must not be uploaded to the public website or repository.

Sharing and third parties

The backup workflow is not intended to sell Google data, use it for advertising or give unrelated third parties access. This informational site is hosted by an external platform, which may process ordinary website access information according to its own policies. No Google Drive files or OAuth secrets are intentionally published on the site.

Revoking access

The account holder can stop the local workflow and revoke access using their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Contact

For questions, use the support email displayed on the OAuth consent screen.
